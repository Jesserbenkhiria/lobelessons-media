## Setup order (per brother)
1. **YouTube:** in the same Google account, create a new channel (Settings → Add or manage channels → Create a channel) and pick the handle.
2. **Instagram:** use "Add account" in the app. Each account needs a new email. Switch it to a Professional (Creator) account.
3. **Facebook:** create a new Page from your profile, then link it to that brother's Instagram account in Meta Business Suite.
4. **TikTok:** each account needs its own email.
5. **Metricool:** add a new brand (Starter plan = 5 brands) and connect those 4 accounts.
6. Upload the profile picture, cover, banner and bio. Then leave the accounts quiet until launch.
