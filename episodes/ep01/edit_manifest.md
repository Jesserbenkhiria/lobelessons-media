# Episode 01: beat-by-beat edit manifest

Every paragraph is mapped to one visual plan. Shot changes land on spoken words, not only on paragraph starts.
**Types:** **STOCK** is a licensed Pexels photo or video with a Ken Burns move or trim. **MG** is a motion graphic (cards, numbers, marker annotations). **LOBE** is a mascot cutaway on a beige or charcoal set.
**Rules:** a visual gag in every section. Lobe appears at transitions, jokes and the three questions only (about 10–20% of runtime). No karaoke captions. Every number comes from `facts.json`.

Status: section 1 is **built** (`story_opening.json`, proof of style). Sections 2–7 are **planned**: the search terms below get sourced once the narration is approved.
